Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers PawClips, the personal publishing tool used by Funny and Cute Animal Vids ("we", "us"). PawClips is used only by its owner to upload our own edited videos to our own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Information we access

When we connect our own accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok), the platform gives PawClips an access token and basic account information, such as the account ID and display name. PawClips uses these only to upload our videos and check that an upload succeeded.

For TikTok specifically, PawClips uses the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish permissions to read basic profile information for our own account and to upload videos to it.

Information we do not collect

PawClips does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers, followers or any other users. It does not read messages, comments, contacts or follower lists, and it does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How information is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are never sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them. Tokens can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings.

Third-party services

Videos are uploaded through the official APIs of YouTube (Google), Facebook and Instagram (Meta), and TikTok. Their own privacy policies apply to content posted on their platforms.

Children

PawClips is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
